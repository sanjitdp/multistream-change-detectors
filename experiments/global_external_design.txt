External global-detection comparison, frozen before simulation (2026-10-06)

This adds external methods to the existing independent-Gaussian ARL study.
K=50, target global-null ARL=1000. Alternatives: means .4 or .75 in the
first 5 or 25 streams beginning at observation 101; variance one; horizon400.
All four alternatives use 2000 runs. Seed50101/50102 for .4 sparse/dense,
50201/50202 for .75 sparse/dense exactly reproduces the existing observations.
Old BH, Bonferroni, parallel CUSUM and Sum-CUSUM results are retained from
the saved paired observations, without refitting them. Both BH and Bonferroni
are labeled ours. Their likelihood increments use the true shift.

Xie--Siegmund (2013), T2, equations3.5--3.7:
max over lengths1,...,min(t,199) of sum over k of
log[1-p0+p0 exp((positive standardized window sum)^2/2)], p0=.1.
The window restriction1<=length<200 and p0=.1 follow their numerical setup.
Post-change means and affected subset are not supplied to this method.
Primary source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1207.2386

Chan (2017), transformed CUSUM, equations5.1--5.3:
R_k,t=max(0,R_k,t-1+mu0 X_k,t-mu0^2/2), mu0=1;
sum_k log[1+p0(lambda_M exp(R_k,t/2)-1)], p0=.1, lambda_M=.64.
These are published numerical choices; they are fixed across alternatives.
Primary source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.08504

Both methods stop at their first global statistic threshold crossing. Neither
is assigned a stream-identification set. Separate independent null training
(400 runs, seed73100) fits each threshold to empirical ARL1000. Calibration
simulates paths until a prespecified upper threshold is crossed and stores
record maxima so lower candidate thresholds can be evaluated on the same
uncensored paths. Upper brackets may be enlarged using training data only.
Starting brackets may use published analytic ARL approximations or a small
timing pilot; all pilot/training outcomes are recorded. Independent null
validation (750 runs, seed74100) continues until every alarm occurs; no
validation-based threshold adjustment. Its actual ARL, standard error and
longest run are reported, even if the ARL differs from1000.

Metrics for all methods: pre-change alarm probability; global detection delay
conditional on no alarm before101; conditional sample size and nondetection
proportion. Delays count observation101 as1. Runs without alarm by400 receive
time401, thus delay301; they are retained. Marginal Monte Carlo standard errors
use independent runs. Existing comparator validation uses1500 runs; new methods
use750. Every method/configuration remains in results regardless of performance.

Performance-only timing precedes large runs. C++ is permitted for the scan.
Numerical verification compares compiled statistics with direct Python window
calculations and checks exact reproduction of saved old first-alarm times.
No manuscript modifications are part of this isolated implementation.
