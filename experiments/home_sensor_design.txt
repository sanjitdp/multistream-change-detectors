Eight MOX resistance channels; intervention onset is time0 in hours.
This is a MODEL-BASED illustration, not empirical validation of EOP or FDR.
Recorded stimulus labels do not identify which individual sensors changed.
Background home activity can contain unlabelled environmental changes.

Chronological selection: include EVERY record dated2015-08-20 or later.
Metadata identifies47 records:29 interventions and18 background recordings.
Earlier records are not used for parameter tuning. Analysis settings are
fixed here and will not be adjusted according to detection outcomes.

Transform: natural logarithm of each of8 positive resistance readings.
Bin into nonoverlapping5-second intervals relative to the recorded onset,
using per-channel medians. Retain[-3600,300)seconds:360 training bins in
[-3600,-1800) and420 monitoring bins in[-1800,300). Assign a bin's result
to its right endpoint,so the first post-onset result is available at5seconds.
Temperature and humidity are retained in source but not modelled.
Rows with nonfinite time/resistance or nonpositive resistance are unavailable.

Predeclared coverage exclusions, independent of evidence or outcomes:
- fewer than300 usable adjacent training-bin pairs in ANY channel;
- missing final training bin (needed as the preceding observation);
- ANY missing monitoring bin in ANY channel;
- for stimulus records,duration shorter than5min.
Missing initial training bins are allowed; OLS uses only observed adjacent
pairs. No interpolation, carry-forward, outcome-based exclusion or time
window changes. Save all selected recordIDs and exclusions with reasons.

For each record and channel, fit an AR(1) with intercept by OLS using only
training-bin pairs. No clipping of fitted coefficient. Compute the median
of these training prediction residuals and subtract it from subsequent
residuals. For each monitoring bin, use the observed immediately preceding
log-resistance,not a recursively simulated prediction. Report coefficients,
training residual medians and held-out residual diagnostics.

Evidence: lambda=.5 fixed in advance. L+ =1+.5sign(residual),
L-=1-.5sign(residual). Directional SR recursions R+=(1+R+)L+ and
R-=(1+R-)L-,both initially0. Two-sided evidence M=(R++R-)/2 is the
exact mixture of the two likelihood products at each possible restart.
K=8,H=420,q=.05,alpha=q/H. Compare current-set declarations:
- e-d-BH(ours): sorted M,threshold K/(alpha*r);
- ParallelSR: each M>=K/alpha;
- Per-streamSR: each M>=1/alpha.
All methods use identical fitted residuals and evidence. Their numerical
calibrations are nominal: residual symmetry conditional on the full past
is not established by OLS,median centering or5-second aggregation.
Accordingly NO finite-sample error guarantee is asserted for this dataset.

Metrics retained for EVERY included record and method:
- any alert during30min before the recorded onset;
- any alert during the complete35min monitoring window;
- any current alert in the first5min after the onset;
- first post-onset alert delay,conditional on its existence,and nondetection;
- first post-onset alert among records without any pre-onset alert,with
  method-specific eligible denominators and nondetection counts;
- fraction of8 channels currently flagged at every monitoring bin.
For background records,time0 is only a reference time,not an intervention.
Report background alert proportions and pre-onset alert proportions as such,
not sensor-level FDR,TDR,or certified false-positive rates.

Diagnostics per channel/record on the held-out PRE-onset residuals:
positive,negative and zero sign proportions;mean sign (sign imbalance);
lag1 residual and sign autocorrelation. These diagnose possible failures
of the conditional-symmetry working model,not proof of validity if small.

Plots fixed in advance: (1) background records with any alert over35min,
and intervention detection by5min among records without a pre-onset alert,
with counts/denominators and '(ours)' label; (2) current fraction of channels
flagged over time,separately for wine,banana and background. Allthree methods
and allthree classes retained. Recordings may be dependent or overlapping;
report counts and descriptive fractions without independent-binomial CIs.
